[Slide 7: Innovation 1 - Structural Re-parameterization (RepConv)]
- Headline: Decoupling Training Complexity from Deployment Latency
- The Paradox: Complex multi-branch networks learn richer representations during training but cause severe memory access costs (MAC) and latency spikes during inference.
- The Algebraic Solution:
  - Training Phase: 3 parallel branches: 3x3 Conv + BN, 1x1 Conv + BN, and Identity + BN.
  - Deployment Phase (switch_to_deploy): BN parameters are fused into convolution weights: W' = gamma/sqrt(sigma^2+eps)*W, b' = beta - gamma*mu/sqrt(sigma^2+eps). 1x1 kernels are zero-padded to 3x3, and Identity is transformed into a Dirac delta kernel.
  - Linear summation collapses all branches into a single equivalent 3x3 Conv: W_fused = W'_3x3 + Pad(W'_1x1) + W'_id.
- Empirical Gain: Forward latency slashed from 7.12 ms down to 2.92 ms on Tesla T4 (5.35 ms on RTX 3050 Laptop) with zero accuracy degradation!

[Slide 8: Innovation 2 - Spatial CoordConv & Sparse BiFormer Attention]
- Headline: Spatial Inductive Priors & Dynamic Token Routing
- Spatial Coordinate Convolution (CoordConv):
  - Appends two hardcoded normalized coordinate channels Cx, Cy in [-1, 1] to the input tensor (640x640x5).
  - Inductive Prior: Explicitly encodes vertical spatial geometry—helmets strictly appear on human heads, while yellow construction clutter resides at ground/floor level.
- Bi-Level Routing Attention (BiFormer):
  - Coarse Region Partitioning: Divides feature maps into S x S regions, computing regional affinity A^r = Q^r (K^r)^T and filtering for Top-k routing indices.
  - Token-level attention is computed strictly across gathered relevant regions, lowering quadratic self-attention complexity O(H^2 W^2) down to linear complexity O(S^2 + k*HW/S^2).
  - Outcome: 100% of compute focuses on tiny, occluded helmet contours while ignoring massive background noise.

[Slide 9: Innovation 3 - Focal EIoU Bounding Box Loss]
- Headline: Overcoming Aspect-Ratio Ambiguity & Small-Target Occlusion
- Limitation of Conventional CIoU: Relies on aspect ratio arctan(w/h) difference, which obscures true width/height errors when objects are partially occluded.
- Focal EIoU Formulation:
  - Explicitly decomposes bounding box error into overlap, center distance, and independent side lengths: L_EIoU = (1 - IoU) + rho^2(b, b_gt)/c^2 + rho^2(w, w_gt)/C_w^2 + rho^2(h, h_gt)/C_h^2.
  - Focal hard-sample modulation: L_Focal-EIoU = IoU^gamma * L_EIoU (with gamma = 0.5).
- Advantage: Accelerates bounding box convergence and delivers razor-sharp localization on tiny helmet boundaries under scaffolding occlusions.
