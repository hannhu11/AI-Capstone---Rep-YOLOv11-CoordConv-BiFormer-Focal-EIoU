SLIDE 7: INNOVATION 1 - STRUCTURAL RE-PARAMETERIZATION (REPCONV)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Headline: Decoupling Training Representational Power from Deployment Latency
- The Structural Dilemma: Multi-branch topologies enrich gradient flow and representation capacity during training, but introduce costly memory access overhead (MAC) and latency spikes during inference.
- Algebraic Branch Fusion (switch_to_deploy):
  * Batch Normalization Fusion: W' = gamma/sqrt(sigma^2+eps)*W; b' = beta - gamma*mu/sqrt(sigma^2+eps)
  * Zero-Padding: 1x1 conv kernels are center-padded to 3x3.
  * Dirac Delta Transform: Identity shortcut is converted into an equivalent 3x3 identity convolution.
  * Additive Collapse: W_fused = W'_3x3 + Pad(W'_1x1) + W'_id
- Empirical Latency Gain: Forward latency slashed from 7.12 ms down to 2.92 ms on Tesla T4 (5.35 ms on RTX 3050 Laptop) with zero accuracy degradation!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 8: INNOVATION 2 - SPATIAL COORDCONV & SPARSE BIFORMER ATTENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Headline: Spatial Inductive Priors & Dynamic Bi-Level Token Routing
- Spatial Coordinate Convolution (CoordConv):
  * Augments input tensor with hardcoded normalized coordinate channels Cx, Cy in [-1, 1] (resulting in 640x640x5).
  * Inductive Prior: Injects vertical spatial geometry—helmets are anatomically constrained to worker head regions, while yellow clutter resides at ground/floor levels.
- Bi-Level Routing Attention (BiFormer):
  * Divides feature maps into S x S regions; computes regional affinity A^r = Q^r (K^r)^T and routes computation to top-k relevant regions.
  * Reduces quadratic self-attention complexity O(H^2 W^2) down to linear complexity O(S^2 + k*HW/S^2).
  * Outcome: Directs 100% of attention to tiny, occluded helmet contours while filtering out 80% of background noise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 9: INNOVATION 3 - FOCAL EIOU BOUNDING BOX REGRESSION LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Headline: Aspect-Ratio Decoupling & Hard-Sample Error Modulation
- Deficiency of Traditional CIoU: Uses aspect ratio arctan(w/h) error, which confounds width and height discrepancies and fails when occlusions distort aspect ratios.
- Mathematical Formulation of Focal EIoU:
  * L_EIoU = (1 - IoU) + rho^2(b, b_gt)/c^2 + rho^2(w, w_gt)/C_w^2 + rho^2(h, h_gt)/C_h^2
  * Separates aspect ratio into independent horizontal and vertical side-length penalties.
  * Focal Modulation: L_Focal-EIoU = IoU^gamma * L_EIoU (gamma = 0.5) to dynamically upweight low-overlap, hard-to-localize small helmets.
- Validation Gain: Rapid bounding box convergence and precise localization around scaffolding occlusions.

- Headline: Zero-Shot Transfer Across 33,000+ Images & Annotation Discrepancy Analysis
- Cross-Domain Generalization Results (Table 3):

  * SHEL5K (5,000 images): 41.15% mAP50 under steep aerial drone nadir views (70–90 deg) and sub-15px targets.
- Mathematical Autopsy of the "IoU Collapse":
  * Root Cause: Annotation ontology mismatch. Source SHWD labels 'person' as Full-Body, while Hard Hat Workers labels 'person' as Head-Only.
  * When detector predicts full body (B_pred), ground-truth head box (B_gt) is nested inside: IoU = A_head / A_body approx 0.07–0.14 << 0.50.
  * Result: Simultaneously penalized as False Positive and False Negative, collapsing joint mAP to 74.40%.
  * Harmonization Proof: When evaluated on Hat-Only, mAP jumps to 97.03%, proving neural representations transfer flawlessly!
