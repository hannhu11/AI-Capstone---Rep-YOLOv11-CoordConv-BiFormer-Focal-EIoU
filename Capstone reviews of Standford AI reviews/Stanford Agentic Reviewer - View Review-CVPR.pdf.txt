CVPR

Summary
The paper proposes Rep-YOLO11s, a real-time helmet detection framework for
construction surveillance that integrates four known components: structural re-
parameterization (RepConv) to fuse multi-branch convolutions at inference,
CoordConv to inject spatial coordinates, BiFormer bi-level routing attention to
focus computation on salient regions, and Focal-EIoU for bounding box
regression. The authors report strong single-split and 5-fold results on SHWD,
extensive zero-shot tests across several external PPE datasets, and multi-platform
deployment benchmarks and pipeline breakdowns that emphasize end-to-end
throughput and timing integrity.

contribution falls short of CVPR standards. I recommend rejection in its current
form; the work would be strengthened by rigorous, fair SOTA baselines under
consistent protocols, clearer and statistically sound ablations, transparent TRT and
pipeline reproducibility, and a more substantive methodological contribution.
